--- title: "Adapting for Adoption: Tailoring HPC Training for Resource-Constrained Communities" authors: - given-name: "Bryan" surname: "Johnston" - given-name: "James" surname: "Carson" - given-name: "Charlie" surname: "Dey" - given-name: "Jennifer M." surname: "Schopf " - given-name: "Christopher J." surname: "Whalen" - given-name: "Grace" surname: "Kebirungi" start-page: 6 end-page: 9 abstract: "Successful High-Performance Computing (HPC) adoption in resource constrained environments requires more than hardware deployment; it requires training models that are explicitly designed for local realities rather than transplanted from well-resourced contexts. This position paper argues that leadership-class HPC curricula must be re-engineered into pragmatic, context-aware training pathways if emerging HPC communities -- particularly in Africa -- are to achieve sustainable operational capability. Drawing on the adaptation of the Texas Advanced Computing Center's (TACC) leadership-class training into an onboarding program for African HPC sites, we illustrate how global best practices can be reframed into actionable modules aligned with infrastructural, staffing, and operational constraints. We contend that such contextualisation is not optional but foundational for long-term HPC sustainability, and we outline principles for designing training that empowers institutions to deploy and operate HPC systems sustainably and effectively within resource-limited environments." keywords: - "HPC Training" - "workforce development" - "resource-constrained environments" - "African HPC" subjects: - "Information Technology" - "Data Science" - "Engineering" audiences: - "Educator" - "Professional/Practitioner" education-levels: - "Graduate/Professional" ---
